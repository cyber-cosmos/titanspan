# Code of Conduct

Titanspan is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussion and review.
- Offer specific, constructive feedback and receive it in good faith.
- Focus disagreements on ideas and evidence.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation,
and publishing another person's private information without permission are
unacceptable.

## Enforcement and reporting

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation that violates this
policy. Report a conduct concern using GitHub's report-content controls or by
contacting a repository administrator through GitHub. Include relevant links
and context, and do not post private information publicly.
